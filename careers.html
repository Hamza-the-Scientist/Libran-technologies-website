Data Analysis Internship

Internship Hybrid / Hyderabad 3 - 6 Months
Open

We are looking for a motivated Data Analysis Intern to join our core technical team. You will work on real-world data analysis.

Key Responsibilities:

    Work with real-world datasets for practical data analysis.
  • Perform data cleaning, organization, and preparation.
  • Conduct basic statistical analysis and interpret results.
  • Create tables, charts, and data visualizations.
  • Learn to identify patterns, trends, and relationships in data.
  • Prepare clear and meaningful data analysis reports.
  • Apply data analysis techniques to academic and professional projects.
  • Participate actively in assigned practical tasks and training sessions.

Requirements:

  • Basic knowledge of computer operation.
  • Interest in data analysis, statistics, research, or related fields.
  • Students or graduates from Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science, Economics, or related disciplines are encouraged to apply.
  • Basic knowledge of Excel is helpful but not mandatory.
  • Willingness to learn and work with real datasets.
  • Commitment to completing assigned tasks and attending training sessions.
  • No advanced programming experience is required; training will be provided.
